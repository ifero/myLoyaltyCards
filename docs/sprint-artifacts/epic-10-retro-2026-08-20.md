## What Went Well

### 1. Every story corrected its own brief instead of following it blindly

- **10-1** found that AGP 9.0 **removed embedded-Wear-app support outright** (the `wearApp`
  configurations are gone). The story assumed a config plugin was merely unnecessary; reality was
  stronger — embedding is unsupported. The standalone-APK decision became structural, not stylistic.
- **10-2** deliberately superseded three of the epic's written acceptance criteria (commit the
  generated file and drift-check it, Node generator rather than `.kts`) following the pattern Story
  5-8 settled.
- **10-3** caught that AC4's "favourites first in every sort mode" contradicted the canonical
  `useCardSort.ts` (favourites are **not** pinned in `recent`) and deferred to the source over the
  prose, with ifero ratifying mid-story.

### 2. The port improved on what it ported from

- **10-2** included the _output_ file's digest in the incremental-skip decision. The Swift generator
  hashes inputs only, which deadlocks a hand-edited artifact: inputs still match so `generate` skips
  and leaves the edit, while `--check` keeps failing. Kotlin side fixed; Swift side flagged.
- **10-2** derived `defaultFormat` validation and the generated KDoc from `core/schemas/card.ts`
  rather than transcribing a parallel list that would go stale silently.
- **10-4** used ZXing instead of porting the ~500 hand-implemented lines of
  `targets/watch/BarcodeGenerator.swift`, and thereby covered **six** formats where watchOS covers
  three.
- **10-3** ported Story 9-5's _review fixes_ (mixed-`null` `frequent` tier, diacritic-insensitive
  A-Z via `Collator` PRIMARY) rather than re-deriving the logic and re-earning the bugs.

### 3. Kotlin tests actually run in CI — better than the watchOS precedent

`wear-os-build.yml` runs `./gradlew testDebugUnitTest assembleDebug` on every qualifying PR. Room DAO,
migration, applier, coordinator and outbox tests all run under Robolectric on the Ubuntu runner with
no emulator. Apple Watch's Swift XCTests have never run in CI — only a TypeScript contract suite. This
is a genuine step up rather than parity.

### 4. Prior lessons applied on day one

**10-5** applied Story 5-9's lesson (one storage surface, Room only) from the start instead of
shipping two surfaces and removing one later, and removed 10-3's in-memory placeholder as part of the
same change.

### 5. Flagged gaps became real stories

The out-of-scope flags did not evaporate. The watchOS barcode-format gap surfaced by 10-4 is now
Story 16-28, alongside 16-26, 16-27 and 16-29 — four watch stories in `ready-for-dev`.

## Challenges

> Systems and process observations. No blame.

### 1. Story 10-6 was flagged as under-scoped three times and shipped un-split

The 2026-07-29 refinement pass said it. The story header repeats it: _"materially larger than 10-1 …
10-5 and should probably be split."_ The story's own out-of-scope section proposes the exact split —
**10-6a** (phone transport module + dispatch seam, testable with a stub watch) and **10-6b** (watch
receive + `CARD_USED` outbox).

It shipped as one story at ≈3.8× the median sibling, and it is where both of the epic's serious bugs
lived. The recorded reason for not splitting: _"the sprint plan lists a single 10-6 and re-keying
stories mid-sprint has its own cost."_ **The tracker's shape drove an engineering decision.**

### 2. Epic 10 shipped without its end-to-end hardware validation

Story 10-6's header states: _"Do not mark this done until @ifero has filled in the two validation
tables."_ Both tables remain unfilled on every row:

- **AC17** — five two-device scenarios: initial sync, add→appears, delete→disappears,
  open→`usageCount` increments, offline opens→queued events flush.
- **AC18** — the Apple Watch hardware re-check (two scenarios).

The story was marked `done` and merged as #209 regardless. This is Epic 9 Action Item #2 recurring one
platform over — that item (_validate on a physical pair, **gates Epic 10**_) was cleared on the
2026-07-20 RC, and Epic 10 then ended in the same state.

**Compounding factor:** `watch-android` has **no crash reporting**. The "0 production incidents" figure
is not evidence of health — Wear OS is currently unobservable in the field, and the bug class in
Challenge 3 fails silently by construction.

**Decision taken at this retro (ifero):** accept the risk and close. See DEC-E10-RETRO-001.

### 3. Two production-class bugs found only by review — neither visible from reading the code

- **Unbounded Wearable API calls on the watch.** The phone half bounded every Play services call at
  10s citing Story 16-10; the watch half did not. `UsageOutbox.flush()` runs inside `Mutex.withLock`,
  and a Kotlin mutex is released when the critical section _completes_ — a coroutine suspended forever
  holds it forever. One `sendMessage` that never settled would have permanently deadlocked every later
  card open and every later reconnection, for the life of the process. `runCatching` is powerless
  because nothing is ever thrown. Fixed with `withTimeout`; `TaskAwaitTest` reproduces the scenario.
- **`Task.addOnCompleteListener(listener)` posts to the main looper.** Writing the test for the above
  exposed it: every Data Layer call on the watch was hopping onto the UI thread purely to resume a
  coroutine already on a background dispatcher. Fixed with the `(Executor, listener)` overload and a
  direct executor; a test asserts resumption happens off the main thread.

### 4. A test-integrity bug — tests that named a branch and never ran it

Two "never throws" tests in `core/wear-connectivity.test.ts` silently exercised only their _first_
scenario. `getNativeModule` requires the native module lazily, so the require ran after
`jest.isolateModules` had exited and resolved in the parent registry; the first `loadModule` cached
there and the second reused it. Istanbul showed **zero hits** on
`isWearTransportSupported`'s catch and `getConnectedWearNodeCount`'s early return, despite tests named
for them. Fixed by splitting each multi-scenario test into one `test()` per case, with a load-bearing
comment at the split.

Same family as the Story 16-15 lesson: **green does not mean covered.**

### 5. The complication follow-up has been carried for four sprints

Sprint 14 action #3 → Epic 9 action #5 (❌ not addressed) → consciously parked into Epic 10 on
2026-07-11 → **all six Epic 10 stories independently flagged that it still has no story number** →
Epic 10 closes without it. 10-2 notes it is the one deliverable that would need a real Wear OS drawable
pipeline plus the per-logo luminance analysis the watchOS widget uses.

### 6. A CI path-filter gap

`wear-os-build.yml` is path-filtered to `watch-android/**`. A PR touching only
`modules/wear-data-layer/` or `core/wear-connectivity.ts` would not trigger it. Partly mitigated by
`core/wear-sync-contract.test.ts`, which runs unfiltered in `ci-quality-gates.yml`, and by the new
`phone-wear-module-tests.yml` job. Widening the trigger paths was deliberately left out of scope as a
CI-policy change.

## Key Decisions

### DEC-E10-RETRO-001: Ship Epic 10 without on-device validation — accepted risk

**Context:** Story 10-6's AC17 (five two-device sync scenarios) and the hardware half of AC18 (Apple
Watch re-check) were never performed; the dev agent has no physical Android phone, Wear OS watch or
Apple Watch. The story instructed that it not be marked done until the tables were filled. It was
merged anyway.
**Decision:** Accept the risk and close Epic 10 rather than hold it as a gate. Recorded as an explicit
accepted risk, not as a pending checkbox, so the record is honest either way.
**Residual risk:** Bluetooth reconnection timing and process death under real memory pressure are the
parts that only fail on real hardware — precisely what AC17 existed to cover. With no crash reporting
on `watch-android`, a field failure produces no signal.
**Mitigation:** Action item 1 (Wear OS field observability).
**Owner:** ifero · **Status:** Agreed 2026-08-20.

### DEC-E10-RETRO-002: Complication re-parked, now with a trigger

**Context:** Carried four sprints with no story number; flagged by all six Epic 10 stories.
**Decision:** Remain parked, but attached to a concrete trigger instead of drifting — **draft the story
when the Wear OS APK is published to Play.** That is the same gate 10-1 flagged for the remaining
release plumbing (Play Console setup, keystore, Digital Asset Links, Fastlane watch lanes), so the
complication work lands with the rest of it rather than alone.
**Owner:** ifero · **Status:** Parked with trigger, 2026-08-20.

### DEC-E10-RETRO-003: Epic 10 closes; Sprint 18 closes

**Context:** All six Epic 10 stories done; all nine Sprint 18 stories done.
**Decision:** `epic-10` → `done`, `epic-10-retrospective` → `done`, Sprint 18 → `completed`. Remaining
work tracked as action items rather than as an open epic.
**Owner:** ifero · **Status:** Agreed 2026-08-20.
