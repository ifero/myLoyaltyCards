CARDÌ — THE WALLET PATTERN, FOUR STATE FRAMES
=============================================

The wallet is the app's front door and the only screen where the thesis is visible: the
home grid already carries 57 third-party brand colours that arrive with the data. Every
decision below exists to keep our chrome out of their way.

One frame per prompt. States are separate frames, never combined.
Frame A (POPULATED) is THE exemplar. The other three are its siblings.

Before generating:
  - Model selector on 3.1 Pro.
  - Seed from an existing corrected-geometry screen where possible. Generation FROM ZERO
    only works in the Stitch web prompt box; `generate_screen_from_text` over MCP times out
    and lands nothing. `generate_variants` from a seed works and returns synchronously
    (though it can time out at the MCP layer and still land — poll, never retry).
  - Stitch cannot emit 393 x 852; it injects `body { min-height: max(884px, 100dvh) }`.
    Treat whatever it returns as the argument, not the artifact, and author the final frame
    into `frames/cardi-form-frames.html`.

Screen source of truth: features/cards/components/CardList.tsx, CardTile.tsx,
EmptyState.tsx; geometry in features/cards/utils/gridLayout.ts; strings in
shared/i18n/locales/en.ts. Every label below is the real one.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT READING THE CODE TURNED UP  (decisions baked into the prompts)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. THE WALLET HAS SIX STATES, NOT TWO. `CardList` branches on loading, error, single-card,
   multi-card grid, empty, and no-results. The single-card state — an enlarged centred tile
   at 220 x 180 with the tip "Tap + to add more cards to your wallet" — has never been
   designed. Loading and error are a spinner and a line of text; they do not earn frames.

2. SEARCH AND SORT APPEAR ONLY AT TWO OR MORE CARDS. `showControls = totalCount >= 2`. So
   the single-card frame has no search bar, and the empty frame has no search bar. Drawing
   one is wrong, not merely extra.

3. THE SCREEN MARGIN HERE IS 16pt, NOT THE DESIGN SYSTEM'S 20px — AND THE TILE MATH DEPENDS
   ON IT. `TILE_WIDTH = 171` is `(390 − 2 x 16 − 16) / 2`, frozen in gridLayout.ts with a
   documented derivation and tests. At 20px margins the tile stops being 171. These prompts
   use 16pt and the design system needs amending to say so: 20px is the SINGLE-COLUMN
   (form / settings / document) margin; the grid is 16. This is now the FOURTH answer to the
   margin question in this codebase — see § Three answers to the screen margin.

4. THE SHIPPED EMPTY STATE VIOLATES THE DESIGN SYSTEM FOUR TIMES. It carries a per-screen
   invented wallet illustration; accent dots in `#FFCC00` (Esselunga's exact yellow) and
   `#E2231A` (Coop's exact red) used as decoration; a coloured GLOW SHADOW under the CTA
   (`shadowOpacity: 0.3, shadowRadius: 12`, elevation 6); and a 240 x 50 centred button
   where the system specifies a full-width 52px one. All four are corrected below.

5. THE EMPTY STATE GETS NO ILLUSTRATION. Wave A is illustration-free, and the system forbids
   inventing an illustration style per screen. The empty state is TYPOGRAPHIC. If it feels
   bare, that is the correct amount of bare until the commissioned set exists.

6. THE CARD TILE HAS A DROP SHADOW IN LIGHT MODE (`shadowColor: '#000'`). The system says no
   drop shadows anywhere. Removed below; hierarchy comes from the brand colour itself.

7. THE HEADER IS ALREADY RIGHT. `HeaderLeft` is `+` -> /add-card, `HeaderRight` is the gear
   -> /settings. The design system's hard rule ratifies shipped behaviour rather than
   inventing it. The title, however, is still `navigation.home: 'myLoyaltyCards'` — under the
   rebrand it reads "Cardì", and that string change belongs to the rename, not the redesign.

8. THE FAVOURITE BADGE NEEDS A NEW TREATMENT. Shipped is an amber star on a 95%-white plate,
   chosen so it survives any tile colour. Amber is not in this system and white plates go
   invisible on light brands. Replaced below with an INK plate carrying a BEAM star: legal
   (icons take ink or beam, never a card accent), and legible on all 57 brand colours
   including Esselunga's yellow.

PROMPT A — POPULATED  (the canonical exemplar)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Design ONE mobile screen at 393x852, light mode: the wallet home screen for Cardì, holding
eight loyalty cards. This is the app's front door and the screen the whole product exists to
serve, so density and calm matter more than any flourish.

There is NO bottom tab bar and NO floating action button. Do not draw either. Do not add an
illustration. No drop shadows, no gradients, no glassmorphism.

GROUND: cream #F0F0E8 across the whole screen.

HEADER
A `+` icon on the LEFT, the title "Cardì" centred, a gear icon on the RIGHT. Both icons are
outline style, 1.5px stroke, ink #181824, each on a 48x48 touch target. Flat — no shadow, no
coloured bar, no divider under it. Title in Space Grotesk 24px bold, ink #181824.

SEARCH
Below the header, a single search field: white #FFFFFF fill, 1px #D6D6CB outline, 12px
radius, 48px height, with an outline magnifier icon in muted #55555F at its left and the
placeholder "Search loyalty cards" in #55555F.

SORT ROW
Directly beneath, one row: on the left "8 cards" in Inter 13px semibold, ink #181824; on the
right a small text button reading "Recently used" in Inter 13px semibold, ink #181824, with
a small outline chevron-down after it. No borders, no pills, no filter chips.

THE GRID — this is the point of the screen
A TWO-COLUMN grid of brand tiles. 16px screen margins, 16px gutter between columns and rows.
Each tile is 171 wide x 140 tall with a 16px radius, and is FILLED EDGE TO EDGE with that
brand's own colour. Centred inside each tile is that brand's logo or wordmark in white, sized
to about 60% of the tile width. Beneath each tile, outside it, the card name in Inter 13px
semibold ink #181824, one line, centred.

Use exactly these eight brands, in this order, with these exact fills:
  1. Esselunga  #FFCC00   (wordmark in ink #181824, NOT white — the fill is too light)
  2. Conad      #DA291C
  3. Coop       #E2231A
  4. Carrefour  #004E9F
  5. Lidl       #0050AA
  6. Eurospin   #0069B1
  7. Pam        #165226
  8. Decathlon  #0082C3

The FIRST tile (Esselunga) is a favourite: it carries a small circular badge in the top-right
corner, inset 8px — an ink #181824 filled circle, 24px, holding a beam #FCCC0C star at 16px.
No other tile has a badge.

TONE AND THE ONE RULE THAT GOVERNS THIS SCREEN
The chrome is quiet so the brands can be loud. Every pixel that is not a tile is cream, ink,
white or hairline grey. The signature yellow #FCCC0C appears ONLY in that one favourite star
— never as a background, a button, a header, a chip or a highlight anywhere on this screen,
because Esselunga's #FFCC00 is effectively the same yellow and a yellow chrome surface next
to that grid would make the tile vanish into the furniture.

Tiles are flat: no shadow, no border, no gloss, no inner glow, no tint or wash over the brand
colour. Do not unify the tiles — their clashing is the content. Do not add an "add card" tile
to the grid, do not add category headers, and do not put a floating button over the grid.

Headings in Space Grotesk, everything else Inter.

PROMPT B — EMPTY  (zero cards, first launch)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Design ONE mobile screen at 393x852, light mode: the wallet home screen for Cardì with NO
cards at all — a person's first launch. Sibling frame to the populated wallet.

There is NO bottom tab bar and NO floating action button. Do not draw either. No drop
shadows, no gradients, no glassmorphism.

GROUND: cream #F0F0E8 across the whole screen.

HEADER
Identical to the populated frame: `+` on the LEFT, title "Cardì" centred, gear on the RIGHT,
outline icons at 1.5px stroke in ink #181824, flat, no divider.

NO SEARCH FIELD AND NO SORT ROW. They do not exist below two cards. Do not draw them, not
even greyed out.

BODY — vertically centred in the space between header and footer.
1. A title "No cards yet" in Space Grotesk 24px bold, ink #181824, centred.
2. Beneath it, two lines of subtitle, centred, Inter 15px, muted #55555F, reading exactly:
   "Add your first loyalty card and"
   "never miss rewards at checkout"

ABSOLUTELY NO ILLUSTRATION. No wallet graphic, no card graphic, no icon, no sparkles, no
dashed outlines, no decorative dots, no empty-box drawing. This screen is TYPE ONLY. The
illustration set for this product does not exist yet, and inventing one here is the specific
failure this instruction exists to prevent.

FOOTER — anchored to the BOTTOM of the frame.
A 1px #D6D6CB hairline rule spanning the full frame width, and below it a full-width button
labelled "Add your first card": ink #181824 fill, white text in Inter 17px semibold, 52px
tall, 12px radius, 16px side margins, sitting above the safe area.

The button is in the page flow: it does not float, it is not a FAB, and it casts NO shadow
and NO coloured glow of any kind. Draw it fully enabled at full ink #181824.

Leave the space between the subtitle and the hairline EMPTY cream #F0F0E8.

TONE
Quiet and unembarrassed. An empty wallet is a beginning, not an error — nothing here is
apologetic, nothing is grey-on-grey, and nothing is decorated to compensate for the
emptiness.

PROMPT C — SINGLE CARD  (exactly one card)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Design ONE mobile screen at 393x852, light mode: the wallet home screen for Cardì holding
EXACTLY ONE card. Sibling frame to the populated wallet. This state exists because a
two-column grid holding a single tile looks broken.

There is NO bottom tab bar and NO floating action button. Do not draw either. Do not add an
illustration. No drop shadows, no gradients, no glassmorphism.

GROUND: cream #F0F0E8 across the whole screen.

HEADER
Identical to the populated frame: `+` on the LEFT, title "Cardì" centred, gear on the RIGHT,
outline icons at 1.5px stroke in ink #181824, flat, no divider.

NO SEARCH FIELD AND NO SORT ROW — they appear only at two or more cards.

BODY
A single ENLARGED tile, horizontally centred, sitting 32px below the header. It is 220 wide x
180 tall with a 20px radius — deliberately larger and rounder than a grid tile. It is filled
edge to edge with Esselunga's #FFCC00, carrying the Esselunga wordmark centred in ink #181824
at about 60% of the tile width. Flat: no shadow, no border, no gloss.

Beneath the tile, centred, the card name "Esselunga" in Inter 13px semibold, ink #181824.

Below that, with 16px of space, one line of muted guidance in Inter 15px, #55555F, centred,
reading exactly: "Tap + to add more cards to your wallet"

Everything below that line is EMPTY cream #F0F0E8, all the way down. There is no footer
button on this screen, no hairline rule, and no second placeholder tile. The `+` in the
header is the only way to add a card, and the tip already points at it.

TONE
One card should feel like a deliberate beginning, not a half-filled grid. The tile is bigger
because it is alone, and the emptiness beneath it is patience, not absence.

PROMPT D — NO RESULTS  (search matches nothing)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Design ONE mobile screen at 393x852, light mode: the wallet home screen for Cardì where the
person has typed a search that matches none of their cards. Sibling frame to the populated
wallet.

There is NO bottom tab bar and NO floating action button. Do not draw either. Do not add an
illustration. No drop shadows, no gradients, no glassmorphism.

GROUND: cream #F0F0E8 across the whole screen.

HEADER
Identical to the populated frame: `+` on the LEFT, title "Cardì" centred, gear on the RIGHT,
outline icons at 1.5px stroke in ink #181824, flat, no divider.

SEARCH — present and ACTIVE
The search field is filled, not placeholder: white #FFFFFF fill, 1px #D6D6CB outline, 12px
radius, 48px height, an outline magnifier icon in #55555F at its left, the typed value
"Ikea" in ink #181824 Inter 17px, and a small outline clear (x) icon in #55555F at its right
end on a 48x48 target.

SORT ROW
Beneath it, one row: on the left "0 cards" in Inter 13px semibold, ink #181824; on the right
the text button "Recently used" in Inter 13px semibold, ink #181824, with a small outline
chevron-down. The row stays visible — the controls do not disappear just because the result
set is empty.

BODY
Below the sort row, a single line of muted text, horizontally centred, sitting about 80px
below the controls, in Inter 15px, #55555F, reading exactly:
  No cards matching "Ikea"

Nothing else. No illustration, no icon, no "try again" button, no suggestions list, no
"clear search" button — the x in the field already does that. Everything below that line is
EMPTY cream #F0F0E8.

TONE
A search miss is not an error and must not be dressed as one. No red, no warning colour, no
sad face, no exclamation. One quiet sentence, and the controls still in reach.
