CARDÌ — THE BARCODE SCREEN, THREE FRAMES
========================================

The hero moment. A person is at a checkout, someone is waiting behind them, and the right
barcode must be on screen and scannable in under three seconds. This is the screen the whole
product exists to serve, and it is the last one to be drawn.

  A. EAN-13  — the exemplar
  B. QR      — the square variant; same screen, different code geometry
  C. NOT FOUND — the error state

Every prompt is self-contained and repeats its own chrome in full. A prompt may NEVER refer to
another frame. Write every correction as a NUMBERED DEFECT.

Screen source of truth: features/cards/screens/BarcodeScreen.tsx, which delegates to
features/cards/components/BarcodeFlash.tsx and BarcodeRenderer.tsx; strings in
shared/i18n/locales/en.ts under `cards.flash`.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT READING THE CODE TURNED UP
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. THE HARD PART IS ALREADY RIGHT, AND IT HAS BEEN TESTED. Pure white `#FFFFFF` field, true
   black `#000000` bars, brightness maximised on mount and restored on unmount, the status
   bar forced to dark-on-white so the screen renders light regardless of theme, and nothing
   overlaying the code — the 200ms animation fades the whole container, never the bars.
   `BarcodeFlash` is named for the use case (flash your card at the till), not a visual
   effect. **Confirmed working at a real checkout by ifero, 2026-08-15.** Nothing in this
   redesign may put that at risk.

2. THE SHADOW AND THE RADIUS GO — BUT NOT BECAUSE THEY SCAN BADLY. `barcodeContainer` carries
   `shadowColor: '#000'`, `shadowOpacity: 0.05`, `shadowRadius: 4`, `elevation: 2`, commented
   "Subtle shadow for definition", plus `borderRadius: 8`. They were suspected of costing
   contrast; the real-checkout test says otherwise, and the shadow renders outside the white
   plate anyway. They are removed for two smaller and honest reasons: the system says no drop
   shadows anywhere, and **a white plate on a white field needs no definition** — the shadow
   is defining a boundary that carries no information. The barcode sits directly on the field.

3. THE WHOLE SCREEN IS OFF-PALETTE. Every colour is from a Tailwind grey ramp, not this
   system: card name and number `#1F2937`, hint `#9CA3AF`, error `#EF4444`, dismiss `#4B5563`.
   None is visible as *wrong* on a white field at full brightness — that is exactly why it
   survived every pass. It is corrected because **this is the screen the design system points
   at when it claims to be real.** If the hero moment is not in the palette, the palette is
   decoration.

4. THIS IS THE ONE SCREEN THAT IS NOT CREAM. Every other frame in this system sits on cream
   `#F0F0E8`. This one is pure white `#FFFFFF`, edge to edge, including both system insets.
   That is not an inconsistency to fix — the white field IS the product feature.

5. THE STORE NAME DOES NOT WEAR OUR DISPLAY FACE. It is set in Inter 20/700, not Space
   Grotesk. Space Grotesk carries *our* personality; the text here is a third-party brand's
   name, and dressing someone else's brand in our voice contradicts the thesis that the
   content is the colour and our chrome stays quiet. Inter is the correct choice, and the
   shipped code already does this — it is ratified, not changed.

6. THE HINT LINE STAYS. "Nothing else on this screen" was written against nav, chrome and
   ads. "Tap anywhere to close" is the only affordance telling someone how to leave a modal
   with no visible dismiss control; removing it would be obedience to the letter of the rule
   at the cost of the person holding the phone.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
COMMON CHROME — true of all three frames
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

GROUND: pure white #FFFFFF across the ENTIRE frame, both system insets included. Not cream,
not #FAFAF2, not off-white. Exactly one background colour and it is #FFFFFF.

SYSTEM INSETS: 59px top carrying the iOS status bar in ink #181824 — "9:41" left in Inter
15px semibold; signal, wifi and battery icons right. 34px bottom carrying a 140 x 5
fully-rounded pill in ink #181824 at 85% opacity, centred. Both sit on white.

NO HEADER. There is no navigation bar, no back chevron, no title and no close button. The
whole screen is the dismiss target.

FORBIDDEN, and this screen is where it matters most: nothing may overlay the bars — no beam,
no scan-line, no shimmer, no watermark, no logo, no gradient, no rounded mask cropping the
code. No drop shadow anywhere, including under the barcode. No card, plate, panel, border or
container around the code. The surround stays neutral — white here, never a saturated field.
Beam #FCCC0C appears NOWHERE on this screen; the real beam comes from the scanner at the till.
No bottom tab bar, no floating action button, no illustration.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROMPT A — EAN-13  (the exemplar)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Design ONE mobile screen at 393x852: the full-screen barcode modal for Cardì, showing an
EAN-13 barcode. Apply the COMMON CHROME above.

Everything is centred on the vertical axis of the frame and centred as a group vertically.

1. The store name "Esselunga" in Inter 20px bold, ink #181824, centred, with 24px beneath it.

2. The barcode: an EAN-13 code, 314 wide x 120 tall, drawn as true black #000000 vertical
   bars of varying widths directly on the white field. No container, no plate, no padding
   box, no border, no radius, no shadow. The bars run to the full 120px height. Nothing
   crosses them.

3. 16px beneath the barcode, the number "2000012345678" in JetBrains Mono 16px, ink #181824,
   letter-spacing 1px, centred.

4. 40px beneath that, the hint "Tap anywhere to close" in Inter 14px, muted #55555F, centred.

Nothing else. No header, no buttons, no icons, no card, no brand logo, no yellow.

TONE
The app's job here is to hold up a perfect white field and get out of the way. Everything on
this screen is either the code, the two labels that identify it, or the one line that says how
to leave. At maximum brightness this should read as a piece of paper, not an interface.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROMPT B — QR  (the square variant)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Design ONE mobile screen at 393x852: the full-screen barcode modal for Cardì, showing a QR
code instead of a linear barcode. Apply the COMMON CHROME above.

Identical composition to the linear version, with one difference: the code is a QR code,
200 x 200, drawn as true black #000000 modules directly on the white field — no container,
no plate, no border, no radius, no shadow, and no logo or icon in its centre.

1. The store name "Farmacia Centrale" in Inter 20px bold, ink #181824, centred, 24px above
   the code.

2. The 200 x 200 QR code, centred.

3. 16px beneath it, "2000012345678" in JetBrains Mono 16px, ink #181824, letter-spacing 1px.

4. 40px beneath that, "Tap anywhere to close" in Inter 14px, muted #55555F, centred.

A QR code is square and a linear barcode is wide; that is the only thing that changes between
these two screens. Do not add a frame, a corner marker, a viewfinder bracket or a centred
logo — every one of those is something drawn on top of a code.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROMPT C — CARD NOT FOUND  (the error state)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Design ONE mobile screen at 393x852: the full-screen barcode modal for Cardì when the card
cannot be loaded. Apply the COMMON CHROME above — including the pure white #FFFFFF ground,
because this screen is still the barcode modal.

Centred vertically and horizontally, with 20px side margins:

1. The message "Card not found" in Inter 17px semibold, #C41E1E, centred.

2. 32px beneath it, "Go back" in Inter 16px, ink #181824, centred — a text link, not a button.
   No fill, no outline, no chevron.

Nothing else. No icon, no warning triangle, no illustration, no sad face, no red background,
no tinted panel. The white field stays white.

TONE
A missing card is a failure of ours, not a catastrophe. `#C41E1E` appears in exactly one place
— the message — and nowhere else. The screen stays as quiet in failure as it is in success.
