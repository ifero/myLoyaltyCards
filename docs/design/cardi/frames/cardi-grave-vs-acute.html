The accent was drawn backwards

Every variant on the sweep-35 sheet used a negative SVG rotation. SVG's y-axis points down, so a negative rotation lifts the right-hand end — an acute. The sheet spelled Cardí from end to end. The name is Cardì: Italian grave, descending left to right.

The fix is one character. This sheet is about the part that isn't automatic: an acute's tall end points right, into the empty space after the ì, while a grave's tall end points left, straight at the d — the tallest letter in the word, whose ascender stem sits on its right side, immediately against the ì. The ruled details below put the ascender, x-height and baseline across both so the clearance can be measured instead of felt.

GRAVE — Cardì

The name. The accent DESCENDS left to right. Its tall end is on the left, so it leans back toward the d — which is why the clearance below has to be checked rather than assumed.
