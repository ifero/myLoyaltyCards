The mark against the wordmark

The mark is the last letter of the wordmark, so they cannot be judged apart. This sheet asks three things: whether the accent is beam or ink when it sits on cream, whether the word itself carries the barcode stem, and how the two lock up. Contrast ratios are measured on the page, not asserted.

Question 1 — beam accent, or ink accent?

Identical wordmarks. The only difference is the colour of the accent. The earlier ruling was ink, because beam on cream fails text-contrast thresholds — but a logotype is not text, and the ruling was never tested at logotype scale.
