CARDÌ — THE CARD DETAIL SCREEN, THREE FRAMES
============================================

The screen you pass through to reach the barcode. It is the only scroll-linked screen in the
app — the header condenses as the hero scrolls away — and the only one where a third-party
brand colour occupies a third of the display.

  A. HERO VISIBLE     — catalogue brand, at rest. The exemplar.
  B. HEADER CONDENSED — scrolled past the hero
  C. CUSTOM CARD      — no brand logo; the first-letter avatar path

Every prompt is self-contained and repeats its own chrome in full. A prompt may NEVER refer to
another frame. Write every correction as a NUMBERED DEFECT.

Screen source of truth: features/cards/screens/CardDetailScreen.tsx and
features/cards/components/CardDetails.tsx, BrandHero.tsx, DetailRow.tsx; strings in
shared/i18n/locales/en.ts under `cards.details`.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT READING THE CODE TURNED UP
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. THE BRAND COLOUR QUESTION SPLITS IN TWO, AND THE ANSWER DIFFERS EACH WAY.
   `headerBg = brand ? brand.color : theme.primary` tints BOTH the navigation header and the
   `BrandHero` band with the brand's own colour, with a contrast-aware foreground.

   - **The hero band keeps it.** On a card's own detail screen the brand colour is CONTENT:
     the hero is the wallet tile enlarged, and that is the thesis working exactly as intended.
   - **The header gives it up.** The header is navigation — a back chevron and a favourite
     toggle. Tinting it makes the app's own chrome wear a third-party brand's colour, which is
     precisely what _"chrome is quiet so the brands can be loud"_ forbids. The header returns
     to cream `#F0F0E8` with ink `#181824` controls, like every other screen in the system.

   Same colour, two verdicts, decided by whether the surface is identifying the card or
   navigating the app.

2. THE CONDENSED HEADER USES A DROP SHADOW. `headerShadowVisible: isHeaderCondensed` turns on
   a shadow once you scroll past the hero. The system forbids drop shadows anywhere; a **1px
   `#D6D6CB` hairline** does the same job and is already the established pattern (the anchored
   footer separates the same way).

3. THE TITLE IS HIDDEN WITH `fontSize: 0`. At rest the header title is `''` at size 0; past
   the hero it becomes the card name at 17. It works, but the frames specify the intent
   rather than the trick: **no title at rest, card name once condensed.**

4. THE FAVOURITE STAR IS `theme.warning` — amber. Same off-palette colour already replaced on
   the wallet tile. It becomes **beam `#FCCC0C`** when favourited and ink outline when not.
   This is one of the very few places beam legitimately appears: an active/selected state.

5. THERE ARE TWO IMPLEMENTATIONS OF THE BARCODE SCREEN, AND THEY HAVE ALREADY DRIFTED.
   `BarcodeFlash` serves the `barcode/[id]` route; `FullscreenBarcode` is opened from inside
   `CardDetails` by tapping the barcode. Both maximise brightness — the critical behaviour is
   intact in both — but the plate differs: `shadowOpacity` 0.05 vs **0.08**, radius 8 vs
   **12**. Both use Tailwind `#1F2937`. **These should be one component.** Not a design task;
   a note for the tokens/layout PR, and the reason `stitch-prompts-barcode.txt` alone does not
   cover the hero moment.

6. THE BARCODE HERE *DOES* GET A WHITE CARD — unlike the fullscreen view. On the fullscreen
   barcode a white plate on a white field carries no information, so it goes. Here the page is
   cream and the barcode is tappable, so the white card is doing two jobs: separating the code
   from the ground, and marking it as interactive. That is the established rule — **white card
   means interactive, bare ground means content.** No shadow either way.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
COMMON CHROME — true of all three frames
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

GROUND: cream #F0F0E8 across the whole frame, both system insets included. The header has NO
background of its own and is never tinted with a brand colour, never #FAFAF2.

SYSTEM INSETS: 59px top carrying the iOS status bar in ink #181824 — "9:41" left in Inter 15px
semibold; signal, wifi and battery icons right. 34px bottom carrying a 140 x 5 fully-rounded
pill in ink #181824 at 85% opacity, centred.

HEADER: 56px, flat. A back chevron on the left on a 48x48 target, ink #181824. A favourite
star on the right on a 48x48 target. No shadow — a 1px #D6D6CB hairline appears beneath it
only when the header is condensed.

MARGINS: 20px, except the hero band which is full-bleed.

FORBIDDEN: bottom tab bars, floating action buttons, drop shadows, gradients, glassmorphism,
illustrations, coral/salmon/terracotta/orange. Beam #FCCC0C appears ONLY as the filled
favourite star — nowhere else on these screens.

PROMPT A — HERO VISIBLE  (the exemplar)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Design ONE mobile screen at 393x852, light mode: the card detail screen for Cardì, at rest
with the hero visible. Apply the COMMON CHROME above.

HEADER: back chevron left in ink #181824, NO title text at all, and on the right a star
outline icon in ink #181824 at 26px (this card is not a favourite). No hairline beneath it.

BODY, no top gap — the hero meets the header:

1. THE HERO: a full-bleed band, edge to edge, 200px tall, filled with Esselunga's own colour
   #FFCC00. Centred inside it, the Esselunga wordmark at about 80px tall in ink #181824 — ink
   and not white, because the fill is light. No gradient, no overlay, no shadow, no rounded
   corners, no inset. The band runs to both frame edges.

2. Below the hero, 20px margins from here down. The card name "Esselunga" in Space Grotesk
   24px bold ink #181824, with 16px above and 16px below.

3. THE BARCODE CARD: a white #FFFFFF container, 16px radius, 1px #D6D6CB outline, NO shadow,
   full width, holding — centred, with 20px of padding — an EAN-13 barcode 280 wide x 100
   tall in true black #000000 on white, and beneath it the hint "Tap to enlarge" in Inter 13px
   muted #55555F. Nothing overlays the bars.

4. 24px below, a white #FFFFFF card, 16px radius, 1px #D6D6CB outline, with three rows
   divided by hairlines. Each row is at least 48px tall with the label on the left in Inter
   15px muted #55555F and the value on the right in Inter 15px ink #181824:
     "Number"  / "2000 0123 4567 8"
     "Color"   / "Yellow"
     "Added"   / "12 August 2026"

5. The section title "MANAGE" in Inter 12px semibold uppercase, letter-spacing 0.05em, muted
   #55555F, 24px above its card. Then a white #FFFFFF card, 16px radius, 1px #D6D6CB outline,
   two rows divided by a hairline:
     "Edit card"   — Inter 17px ink #181824, with a chevron-right
     "Delete card" — Inter 17px #C41E1E, NO chevron. Text only: no red fill, no red border.

TONE
The brand owns the top of this screen and nothing else. Below the hero everything is cream,
white cards and ink — the same furniture as settings. The one red is the delete row; the one
yellow is Esselunga's own, and it stops at the bottom edge of the hero.

PROMPT B — HEADER CONDENSED  (scrolled past the hero)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Design ONE mobile screen at 393x852, light mode: the card detail screen for Cardì, scrolled so
the hero has gone and the header has condensed. Apply the COMMON CHROME above.

HEADER: back chevron left in ink #181824; the card name "Esselunga" centred in Inter 17px
semibold ink #181824; a star outline icon right in ink #181824. Beneath the header, a 1px
#D6D6CB hairline spanning the full frame width. The header background is cream #F0F0E8 — NOT
Esselunga yellow, NOT tinted, NOT a near-white.

There is NO drop shadow under this header. The hairline is what marks the boundary.

BODY, 20px margins. The hero is scrolled away and not visible at all. The content begins
directly beneath the hairline with 16px of space:

1. THE BARCODE CARD: white #FFFFFF, 16px radius, 1px #D6D6CB outline, no shadow, full width,
   holding a centred EAN-13 barcode 280 wide x 100 tall in true black #000000 on white with
   20px padding, and the hint "Tap to enlarge" in Inter 13px muted #55555F beneath it.

2. 24px below, a white card with three hairline-divided rows: "Number" / "2000 0123 4567 8",
   "Color" / "Yellow", "Added" / "12 August 2026" — labels Inter 15px muted #55555F, values
   Inter 15px ink #181824.

3. "MANAGE" in Inter 12px semibold uppercase, letter-spacing 0.05em, muted #55555F, then a
   white card with "Edit card" in ink with a chevron and "Delete card" in #C41E1E with no
   chevron.

No yellow anywhere on this frame. Once the hero is gone the brand is gone with it, and the
screen is indistinguishable in furniture from settings.

PROMPT C — CUSTOM CARD  (no brand logo)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Design ONE mobile screen at 393x852, light mode: the card detail screen for Cardì for a card
the person created themselves, which has no brand logo. Apply the COMMON CHROME above.

HEADER: back chevron left in ink #181824, no title, and on the right a FILLED star in beam
#FCCC0C at 26px — this card IS a favourite. This is one of the very few places beam appears in
the app; it marks an active state and nothing else on the screen is yellow. No hairline.

BODY:

1. THE HERO: a full-bleed band, edge to edge, 200px tall, filled with the card accent
   #0C843C green. Centred inside it, an 80px fully-round circle filled
   rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16) holding the single letter "F" in white, Space Grotesk 28px bold.
   No logo — this card has none. No gradient, no shadow, no rounded band corners.

2. Below the hero, 20px margins. The card name "Farmacia Centrale" in Space Grotesk 24px bold
   ink #181824, 16px above and below.

3. THE BARCODE CARD: white #FFFFFF, 16px radius, 1px #D6D6CB outline, no shadow, holding a
   centred EAN-13 barcode 280 x 100 in true black on white with 20px padding, and "Tap to
   enlarge" in Inter 13px muted #55555F beneath.

4. A white card with three hairline-divided rows: "Number" / "2000 0123 4567 8", "Color" /
   "Green", "Added" / "12 August 2026".

5. "MANAGE", then a white card with "Edit card" in ink with a chevron and "Delete card" in
   #C41E1E with no chevron.

The five accent colours exist for exactly this case — a card with no brand of its own. Use
#0C843C at that value; do not tint, darken or gradient it, and do not put a logo, icon or
pattern in the band. A letter on a colour is the whole design.
