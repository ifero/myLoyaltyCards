CARDÌ — THE CARD DETAIL SCREEN, FOUR FRAMES
===========================================

The screen you pass through to reach the barcode. It is the only scroll-linked screen in the
app, and the frames exist mainly to pin down that transition.

  A. AT REST     — header IS the brand colour, seamless with the hero. The exemplar.
  B. BLENDING    — hero gone, header fading out over cream, title fading in
  C. CONDENSED   — cream plus a hairline, title settled
  D. CUSTOM CARD — no brand logo; a dark accent, so the controls flip to white

Every prompt is self-contained and repeats its own chrome in full. A prompt may NEVER refer to
another frame. Write every correction as a NUMBERED DEFECT.

Screen source of truth: features/cards/screens/CardDetailScreen.tsx and
features/cards/components/CardDetails.tsx, BrandHero.tsx, DetailRow.tsx; contrast helper in
shared/theme/luminance.ts; strings in shared/i18n/locales/en.ts under `cards.details`.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT READING THE CODE TURNED UP
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. THE HEADER FOLLOWS THE BRAND — AS A TRANSITION, NOT A TINT.

   `headerBg = brand ? brand.color : theme.primary` tints both the navigation header and the
   `BrandHero` band. An earlier draft of this spec took the colour OFF the header on the
   grounds that navigation should not wear a third-party brand's colour. That was wrong: it
   cut a horizontal cream band straight across the top of the hero, so the brand block read as
   interrupted rather than as one field.

   What that argument was actually protecting against is narrower than the rule it produced:
   **brand-coloured chrome with nothing beneath it to identify.** That only happens once the
   hero has scrolled away. So the colour is a transition:

     at rest    the header IS the brand colour, and so is the status-bar inset. The field runs
                unbroken from the top of the screen to the bottom of the hero.
     blending   once the hero has gone, the brand fades out over cream and the title fades in.
     condensed  cream plus a hairline. Nothing is left for the colour to identify.

   The endpoints stay clean — full brand, or full cream — so this never becomes forty-five
   permanent pastel tints. The mid-state is one frame of a transition, not a resting style.

2. THE ORDER OF THE TRANSITION MATTERS. The header stays matched to the hero for as long as
   ANY of the hero is visible: they are one field, and a header a shade paler than the band
   directly beneath it reads as a rendering fault, not a transition. The blend belongs to the
   tail of the scroll, after the band has gone. Drafted the other way round it looked broken.

3. "ONE UNBROKEN FIELD" IS A FACT ABOUT THE MARKUP, NOT ABOUT THE COLOUR VALUES.
   The first HTML draft gave the status-bar inset, the header and the hero each their own
   background, every one set to the same brand colour. It rendered with faint horizontal lines
   at both boundaries — visible enough that ifero read them as navigation-bar borders. Nothing
   drew a border. The element edges land on fractional device pixels (measured at .391 on a 2x
   display), so a hairline of the PARENT's cream leaked between siblings that agreed with each
   other but not with what was behind them. A computed `outline: 1.5px` on every element was
   checked and cleared — `outline-style: none`, so it never paints.

   The fix is structural rather than a tweak: ONE element paints the field and the three sit on
   it transparently. Then there is no second colour in that region left to leak. The seam was
   possible only because "three things that match" is not the same object as "three things on
   one field", and the markup was saying the first.

   **This is a live hazard in the shipped app, not only in the frames.** `headerBg` tints the
   navigation header and `BrandHero` as two separately-filled views; any layout PR that keeps
   them separate inherits the same seam. The band wants one filled parent.

4. THE CONTROLS FLIP WITH LUMINANCE, AND THE HELPER IS ALMOST RIGHT.
   `shared/theme/luminance.ts` returns `#FFFFFF` below 0.5 luminance and `#1F1F24` above — so
   ink on Esselunga's `#FFCC00` (luminance ≈ 0.79) and white on the `#0C843C` accent
   (≈ 0.40). Correct behaviour, but `#1F1F24` is **not** ink `#181824`. Another near-palette
   value; it should be ink.

5. THE CONDENSED HEADER USES A DROP SHADOW. `headerShadowVisible: isHeaderCondensed`. The
   system forbids drop shadows anywhere; a **1px `#D6D6CB` hairline** does the same job and is
   already the established pattern for the anchored footer.

6. THE TITLE IS HIDDEN WITH `fontSize: 0`. At rest the title is `''` at size 0; past the hero
   it becomes the card name at 17. These frames specify the intent instead: **no title at
   rest, card name once condensed** — and in Inter, not Space Grotesk, because it is a
   third-party brand's name doing navigation duty and the display face is ours.

7. THE FAVOURITE STAR IS `theme.warning` — amber. The same off-palette colour already replaced
   on the wallet tile. It becomes **beam `#FCCC0C`** when filled, outline in the foreground
   colour when not. One of the very few places beam legitimately appears: an active state.

8. THERE ARE TWO IMPLEMENTATIONS OF THE BARCODE SCREEN, AND THEY HAVE ALREADY DRIFTED.
   `BarcodeFlash` serves the `barcode/[id]` route; `FullscreenBarcode` opens from inside
   `CardDetails` by tapping the barcode. Both maximise brightness — the critical behaviour is
   intact in both — but `shadowOpacity` is 0.05 vs **0.08** and radius 8 vs **12**, and both
   use Tailwind `#1F2937`. **These should be one component.** Not a design task; a note for the
   tokens and layout PRs.

9. THE BARCODE GETS A WHITE CARD HERE, AND NO PLATE AT ALL ON THE FULLSCREEN VIEW. It looks
   like a contradiction and is not. On cream, and tappable, the card separates the code and
   marks it interactive. On a white field a white plate defines a boundary carrying no
   information. **White card means interactive; bare ground means content.**


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
COMMON CHROME — true of all four frames
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The page beneath the header is always cream #F0F0E8. The header and the top inset take the
colour named by each frame — brand, blend or cream — and they always match each other.

SYSTEM INSETS: 59px top carrying the iOS status bar — "9:41" left in Inter 15px semibold,
then signal, wifi and battery icons right, all in that frame's FOREGROUND colour. 34px bottom
on cream, carrying a 140 x 5 fully-rounded pill in ink #181824 at 85% opacity, centred.

HEADER: 56px, flat. A back chevron on the left on a 48x48 target and a favourite star on the
right on a 48x48 target, both in that frame's foreground colour. NEVER a drop shadow — a 1px
#D6D6CB hairline, and only when condensed.

MARGINS: 20px, except the hero band which is full-bleed to both frame edges.

FORBIDDEN: bottom tab bars, floating action buttons, drop shadows, gradients, glassmorphism,
illustrations, coral/salmon/terracotta/orange. Beam #FCCC0C appears ONLY as a filled favourite
star.

THE CONTENT STACK, identical on every frame below the hero, at 20px margins:
  - a white #FFFFFF card, 16px radius, 1px #D6D6CB outline, NO shadow, holding a centred
    EAN-13 barcode 280 wide x 100 tall in true black #000000 on white with 20px of padding,
    and beneath it "Tap to enlarge" in Inter 13px muted #55555F;
  - 24px gap, then a white card with three hairline-divided rows, labels in Inter 15px muted
    #55555F on the left and values in Inter 15px ink #181824 on the right:
      "Number" / "2000 0123 4567 8"   ·   "Color" / <the card's colour name>   ·
      "Added" / "12 August 2026";
  - "MANAGE" in Inter 12px semibold uppercase, letter-spacing 0.05em, muted #55555F;
  - a white card with "Edit card" in Inter 17px ink #181824 with a chevron-right, and
    "Delete card" in Inter 17px #C41E1E with NO chevron — text only, no red fill or border.

PROMPT A — AT REST  (the exemplar)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Design ONE mobile screen at 393x852, light mode: the card detail screen for Cardì at rest,
hero fully visible. Apply the COMMON CHROME above.

FOREGROUND COLOUR for this frame: ink #181824 — Esselunga's yellow is light.

1. THE TOP INSET AND THE HEADER ARE BOTH FILLED #FFCC00, Esselunga's own colour, and so is the
   hero beneath them. There must be NO seam, NO divider and NO change of tone between the
   status bar, the header and the hero: one unbroken field of #FFCC00 from the very top of the
   screen down to the bottom of the hero band. Paint it as ONE filled region
   containing all three, not three regions that happen to share a value: three separately
   filled boxes leave visible hairlines where they meet. The status-bar glyphs and
   both header icons sit on it in ink #181824.

2. NO TITLE in the header at rest. Back chevron left, star OUTLINE right, nothing between them.

4. THE HERO: full-bleed, edge to edge, 200px tall, #FFCC00, with the Esselunga wordmark
   centred at about 32px in ink #181824 — ink, not white, because the fill is light. No
   gradient, no overlay, no shadow, no rounded corners, no inset.

5. Below the hero the page is cream #F0F0E8. The card name "Esselunga" in Space Grotesk 24px
   bold ink #181824, 16px above and below. Then THE CONTENT STACK, with "Color" reading
   "Yellow".

TONE
The brand owns the top of the screen as one continuous block and stops dead at the bottom of
the hero. Everything below is cream, white cards and ink — the same furniture as settings.

PROMPT B — BLENDING  (hero gone, header fading)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Design ONE mobile screen at 393x852, light mode: the card detail screen for Cardì mid-scroll,
caught at the moment the hero has just left and the header is fading from the brand colour to
cream. Apply the COMMON CHROME above.

FOREGROUND COLOUR for this frame: ink #181824.

1. THE TOP INSET AND THE HEADER ARE BOTH FILLED #F8DE74 — Esselunga's #FFCC00 at half
   strength over cream #F0F0E8. This is a transient blend, halfway through a fade, not a
   resting colour. Paint it as ONE filled region
   containing both, not three regions that happen to share a value: three separately
   filled boxes leave visible hairlines where they meet.

2. THE TITLE "Esselunga" is centred in the header in Inter 17px semibold ink #181824, at HALF
   opacity — it is fading in.

4. Back chevron left, star OUTLINE right, both ink #181824. NO hairline beneath the header yet
   — the rule belongs to the condensed state.

5. NO HERO. It has scrolled away entirely; there is no band, no sliver and no yellow anywhere
   below the header. The page beneath is cream #F0F0E8 and THE CONTENT STACK begins 16px under
   the header, with "Color" reading "Yellow".

Do not show any part of the hero on this frame. The header only begins to blend once the band
has gone — while any hero is visible the header matches it exactly.

PROMPT C — CONDENSED  (settled)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Design ONE mobile screen at 393x852, light mode: the card detail screen for Cardì scrolled and
settled, the header fully condensed. Apply the COMMON CHROME above.

FOREGROUND COLOUR for this frame: ink #181824.

1. THE TOP INSET AND THE HEADER ARE BOTH CREAM #F0F0E8 — the fade has completed. No yellow
   remains anywhere on this screen. Not #FAFAF2, not a near-white, not a tint: cream.

2. THE TITLE "Esselunga" centred in Inter 17px semibold ink #181824, at FULL opacity.

4. Back chevron left, star OUTLINE right, both ink #181824. Beneath the header, a 1px #D6D6CB
   HAIRLINE spanning the full frame width. There is NO drop shadow under this header; the
   hairline is what marks the boundary.

5. No hero. THE CONTENT STACK begins 16px below the hairline, with "Color" reading "Yellow".

Once the hero is gone the brand is gone with it, and the screen is indistinguishable in
furniture from settings. That is the intended end state.

PROMPT D — CUSTOM CARD  (dark accent, controls flip to white)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Design ONE mobile screen at 393x852, light mode: the card detail screen for Cardì at rest, for
a card the person created themselves, which has no brand logo. Apply the COMMON CHROME above.

FOREGROUND COLOUR for this frame: WHITE #FFFFFF — the green accent is dark, so every control
and glyph on it flips.

1. THE TOP INSET AND THE HEADER ARE BOTH FILLED #0C843C, the green card accent, and so is the
   hero beneath them: one unbroken field from the top of the screen to the bottom of the band,
   with no seam. Paint it as ONE filled region
   containing all three, not three regions that happen to share a value: three separately
   filled boxes leave visible hairlines where they meet. The status-bar glyphs and the back
   chevron sit on it in WHITE #FFFFFF.

2. NO TITLE at rest. Back chevron left in white. On the right, a FILLED star in beam #FCCC0C —
   this card IS a favourite. That star is the only yellow on the screen.

4. THE HERO: full-bleed, 200px tall, #0C843C, holding a centred 80px fully-round circle filled
   rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16) with the single letter "F" in white, Space Grotesk 28px bold. No
   logo — this card has none. No gradient, no shadow, no rounded band corners.

5. Below the hero the page is cream #F0F0E8. The card name "Farmacia Centrale" in Space Grotesk
   24px bold ink #181824, 16px above and below. Then THE CONTENT STACK, with "Color" reading
   "Green".

The five accent colours exist for exactly this case — a card with no brand of its own. Use
#0C843C at that value; do not tint, darken or gradient it, and do not put a logo, icon or
pattern in the band. A letter on a colour is the whole design.
