CARDÌ — THE SETTINGS PATTERN, SIX FRAMES
========================================

Settings is the app's second-largest surface and the one nobody has counted. The plan called
it "settings list (2 screens)". The code holds one screen with two account states, a separate
language list, and EIGHT bottom sheets — which reduce to four shapes.

Frame A (SETTINGS, SIGNED IN) is the exemplar. B is its account fork. C-F are the four sheet
shapes, one frame each; every other sheet in the app is one of them with different words.

Before generating:
  - Model selector on 3.1 Pro.
  - Stitch cannot emit 393 x 852 — it injects `body { min-height: max(884px, 100dvh) }`.
    Explore there if its ideas are wanted; author the final frame in HTML.
  - When a literal colour keeps losing to the theme, NAME THE COMPONENT and forbid the value
    by hex ("the header has NO background of its own; do NOT use #FAFAF2"). That phrasing has
    worked every time; repeating the literal alone has not.

Screen source of truth: features/settings/screens/SettingsScreen.tsx, its four Section
components, and the eight *Sheet.tsx components; primitives in shared/components/ui
(BottomSheet, ActionRow, Button, TextField); strings in shared/i18n/locales/en.ts. Every
label below is the real one.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT READING THE CODE TURNED UP
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. EIGHT SHEETS, FOUR SHAPES. Fingerprinted by button count and whether they map a list:
     - PICK ONE FROM A LIST (no buttons, renders a list): ThemePickerSheet, LanguagePickerSheet
     - CONFIRM (two buttons): ExportConfirmationSheet, SignOutSheet, ImportPreviewSheet,
       and DeleteAccountSheet step 1
     - ACKNOWLEDGE (one button): ExportEmptyStateSheet, ImportErrorSheet
     - TYPE-TO-CONFIRM (two buttons + a text input): DeleteAccountSheet step 2
   `DeleteAccountSheet` is a TWO-STEP sheet — `useState<1 | 2>` — which is why it has four
   buttons. Step 2 gates its destructive button on `confirmationText === 'DELETE'`. It is the
   only sheet in the app with an input inside it, and the most destructive control we own.

2. THE CHROME IS ALREADY A COMPONENT. Every sheet is built on `BottomSheet` from
   shared/components/ui, which has its own stories and tests. Rows are `ActionRow`, also with
   stories. So unlike the wallet, this pattern is constrained by an existing API — `prefix`
   icon, `label`, `value`, `variant`, `onPress` — and the design must fit it rather than
   invent a row.

3. A FIFTH ANSWER TO THE SCREEN MARGIN. `SettingsScreen` uses `paddingHorizontal: 24`. The
   running tally is `CardForm` 32 · `AuthScreenLayout` token · design system 20 · card grid
   16 · settings 24. **These frames use 20**, and the code should move 24 -> 20. That is the
   opposite call from the grid, for a stated reason: 16 is DERIVED (the 171px tile depends on
   it, with tests), whereas 24 has no derivation behind it and no test pinning it.

4. THE DESTRUCTIVE BUTTON DOES NOT MATCH THE SYSTEM. The system says destructive is
   borderless, `#C41E1E` text, trailing icon. `Button.tsx` renders it as a FILLED
   `theme.error` block with white text and a border. So sign-out and delete are solid red
   slabs today. **These frames follow the system, not the code.** Worth knowing separately:
   the variant is implemented as an unguarded fall-through — `primary`, `secondary` and
   `tertiary` each get an `if`, and everything else returns error-red — so any variant added
   to that union in future silently becomes destructive.

5. THE ACCOUNT SECTION HAS NO SECTION TITLE. `settings.sections` holds exactly three strings —
   Preferences, About, Data. The account block sits at the top with no uppercase header above
   it, which is correct: it is identity, not a category.

6. THE ACCOUNT SECTION FORKS ON AUTH STATE. `AccountSection` and `AccountSectionGuest` are
   different components, not one component with a flag. Guest is a title plus body plus two
   actions; signed-in is an email plus three actions including two destructive ones.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
COMMON CHROME — true of every frame below
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

GROUND: cream #F0F0E8 across the WHOLE frame, including both system insets. There is exactly
one background colour on the screen and it is #F0F0E8. The header has NO background of its
own — no surface, no panel, no tint, no near-white, and specifically never #FAFAF2.

SYSTEM INSETS: 59px top, 34px bottom, both on cream. The top inset carries the iOS status bar
in ink #181824 — "9:41" left in Inter 15px semibold; signal, wifi and battery icons right.
The bottom inset carries a 140 x 5 fully-rounded pill in ink #181824 at 85% opacity, centred.

HEADER: 56px tall, flat, NO divider and no shadow beneath it. A back chevron on the left on a
48 x 48 target, the title centred in Space Grotesk 24px bold ink #181824, nothing on the right.

MARGINS: 24px. Section gap 24px. Row gap 8px.

COLOURS ARE LITERAL HEXES, NEVER TOKENS. Write #181824, #FCCC0C, #F0F0E8, #FFFFFF,
#D6D6CB, #55555F and #C41E1E exactly as written. Do NOT reach for a semantic colour name
instead — not `primary`, not `on-surface`, not `on-background`, not `surface`, not `outline`,
not `outline-variant`, and not a Tailwind utility built on one such as text-primary,
bg-primary, border-primary or border-outline-variant. In this project's theme `primary`
resolves to #000000 and `on-surface` to #1a1c18, so a screen assembled out of tokens comes
out in two wrong near-blacks that look almost right and are not. A literal hex survives; a
token does not.

FORBIDDEN EVERYWHERE: bottom tab bars, floating action buttons, drop shadows, gradients,
glassmorphism, illustrations, coral/salmon/terracotta/orange. Beam #FCCC0C appears nowhere in
this pattern at all — settings has no favourite, no selection accent that earns it, and a
yellow surface here would be the loudest thing in the app for no reason.

PROMPT A — SETTINGS, SIGNED IN  (the exemplar)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Design ONE mobile screen at 393x852, light mode: the Settings screen for Cardì, for a person
who is signed in. Apply the COMMON CHROME above. Title: "Settings".

Single column, 24px margins, sections separated by 24px.

1. ACCOUNT BLOCK — first, and with NO uppercase section header above it.
   A white #FFFFFF container, 16px radius, 1px #D6D6CB outline, holding:
   - the person's email "andrea@example.com" in Inter 17px ink #181824, with a small
     "Signed in" label above it in Inter 12px semibold uppercase, letter-spacing 0.05em,
     muted #55555F;
   - a hairline #D6D6CB rule, then a row "Change Password" in Inter 17px ink #181824 with a
     small outline chevron-right at its right end;
   - a hairline rule, then "Sign Out" in Inter 17px, #C41E1E, no chevron;
   - a hairline rule, then "Delete Account" in Inter 17px, #C41E1E, no chevron.
   Both destructive rows are TEXT ONLY — no red fill, no red border, no red background.

2. SECTION "PREFERENCES" — the title in Inter 12px semibold, uppercase, letter-spacing
   0.05em, muted #55555F, sitting 8px above its rows. Two rows, each 48px min height, in a
   white #FFFFFF container with 16px radius and a 1px #D6D6CB outline, separated by a
   hairline:
   - a 24px outline brightness icon in ink, the label "Theme" in Inter 17px ink #181824,
     and at the right end the current value "System" in Inter 17px muted #55555F followed by
     a small outline chevron-right;
   - a 24px outline language icon, the label "Language", value "System (English)", chevron.

3. SECTION "DATA" — same treatment. Three rows:
   - "Export Data", value "JSON", chevron;
   - "Import Data", chevron, no value;
   - "Sync Now", value "Synced", chevron.

4. SECTION "ABOUT" — same treatment. Four rows:
   - "Help & FAQ", chevron;
   - "Privacy Policy", chevron;
   - "App Version", value "1.4.0", NO chevron — it is information, not a control;
   - "Catalogue", value "2026.08", NO chevron.

Rows that show a value but do nothing must not look tappable: no chevron on them.

TONE
Quiet and dense. This is a utility screen: white cards on cream, ink type, hairline rules,
flat throughout. The only colour on the whole screen is #C41E1E on the two destructive rows.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROMPT B — SETTINGS, GUEST  (the account fork)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Identical to Frame A in every respect — the same COMMON CHROME, the same Preferences, Data
and About sections with the same rows — EXCEPT the account block, which is replaced.

The account block becomes a white #FFFFFF container, 16px radius, 1px #D6D6CB outline,
containing, centred:
- the title "Not signed in yet" in Space Grotesk 20px bold, ink #181824;
- beneath it, the body "Sign in or create an account to back up your cards and sync across
  devices." in Inter 15px, muted #55555F, wrapping to two or three lines;
- beneath that, two full-width buttons stacked with 8px between them:
    "Create Account" — ink #181824 fill, white text, 52px tall, 12px radius;
    "Sign In" — transparent with a 1px ink #181824 outline, ink text, 52px tall, 12px radius.

There is NO email, NO "Change Password", NO "Sign Out" and NO "Delete Account" on this frame —
a guest has no account to act on. There is no red anywhere on this screen.

PROMPT C — SHEET SHAPE 1: PICK ONE FROM A LIST
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Design ONE mobile screen at 393x852, light mode: the Settings screen from Frame A, dimmed
behind a bottom sheet — the theme picker.

BEHIND: Frame A exactly as specified, but covered by a scrim of ink #181824 at 40% opacity
across the entire frame including both insets. The status bar glyphs stay visible above the
scrim in ink; do not lighten or hide them.

THE SHEET: anchored to the bottom edge, full width, white #FFFFFF, with a 16px radius on its
TOP two corners only and square bottom corners. It sits above the 34px bottom inset, and the
home indicator sits ON the sheet in ink #181824 at 85%.

Inside the sheet, 24px margins:
- a grabber at the very top, centred: 36 x 4, fully rounded, #D6D6CB, with 8px above it;
- the title "Theme" in Space Grotesk 20px bold ink #181824, left-aligned;
- three option rows, each 48px min height, separated by a hairline #D6D6CB rule:
    "Light"   — no icon
    "Dark"    — no icon
    "System"  — SELECTED: the label in ink #181824 and a 20px outline check mark in ink
                #181824 at the right end. Only the selected row has a check.
- beneath the last row, a single line of helper text in Inter 13px muted #55555F:
  "System uses your device's appearance setting".

NO buttons on this sheet — no Cancel, no Done, no Apply. Choosing a row IS the action and
dismisses the sheet; adding a button would invent a step the code does not have.

PROMPT D — SHEET SHAPE 2: CONFIRM
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Design ONE mobile screen at 393x852, light mode: the Settings screen from Frame A behind a
40%-ink scrim, with a bottom sheet asking the person to confirm signing out.

Same sheet chrome as Frame C: bottom-anchored, full width, white #FFFFFF, 16px radius on the
top corners only, grabber, 24px margins, home indicator on the sheet.

Inside:
- the title "Sign Out?" in Space Grotesk 20px bold, ink #181824;
- the body "You will return to guest mode. Your cards will remain on this device." in Inter
  15px, muted #55555F, wrapping to two lines;
- 24px of space, then two full-width buttons stacked with 8px between them:
    "Sign Out" — the destructive action. BORDERLESS, no fill, no outline, label in #C41E1E,
                 Inter 17px semibold, 52px tall. It is a TEXT button, not a red slab.
    "Cancel"   — transparent with a 1px ink #181824 outline, ink text, 52px tall, 12px radius.

The destructive action sits ABOVE Cancel, and Cancel is the one with visible structure — the
bordered control is the safe one. Do not give the destructive button a red fill, a red
border, or a red background of any kind.

PROMPT F — SHEET SHAPE 4: TYPE-TO-CONFIRM
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Design ONE mobile screen at 393x852, light mode: the Settings screen from Frame A behind a
40%-ink scrim, with the second step of the delete-account sheet. This is the most destructive
control in the product and it should feel deliberate, not dramatic.

Same sheet chrome as Frame C.

Inside:
- the title "Confirm Account Deletion" in Space Grotesk 20px bold, ink #181824;
- the body "Type DELETE to confirm permanent account deletion." in Inter 15px, muted #55555F;
- the label "TYPE DELETE TO CONFIRM" in Inter 12px semibold uppercase, letter-spacing 0.05em,
  ink #181824, sitting ABOVE its input;
- a text input: white #FFFFFF fill, 1px #D6D6CB outline, 12px radius, 48px minimum height,
  EMPTY, with no placeholder text inside it;
- 24px of space, then two full-width buttons stacked with 8px between them:
    "Delete Account" — destructive and currently DISABLED because the field is empty:
                       borderless, no fill, label in #C41E1E at 40% opacity, Inter 17px
                       semibold, 52px tall. This is the ONE place in this system where a
                       control is drawn disabled, because the gate is the whole point.
    "Cancel"         — transparent with a 1px ink #181824 outline, ink text, 52px tall.

The input is empty and the destructive button is therefore inert. Do not pre-fill the field,
do not show a red outline on it, and do not add a warning banner, an alert icon or a red
background to the sheet. The restraint is what makes the typed word mean something.
