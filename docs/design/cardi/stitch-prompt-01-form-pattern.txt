Design ONE mobile screen at 393x852, light mode: the "Add Card — details" form for Cardì. This screen is the app's form pattern; seven other screens will be derived from it, so the field rhythm matters more than any flourish.

There is NO bottom tab bar and NO floating action button on this screen. Do not draw either. Do not add an illustration.

HEADER
A back chevron on the left, the title "Add Card" centred, nothing on the right. Flat — no shadow, no coloured bar.

BODY — single column, 20px side margins, 8px-grid vertical rhythm, plenty of air between fields.

1. Field "Store Name". A small bold label sitting ABOVE the input (never a floating label). Text input below it, 12px radius, 1px hairline outline, 48px min height, placeholder "Enter store name".

2. Field "Card Number". Same label-above treatment. Text input with placeholder "Enter barcode number", and a separate small square outline button pinned inline at its right end containing a camera icon — a 48x48 touch target for scanning instead of typing.

3. A read-only row labelled "Barcode Format" showing the value "EAN-13 — auto-detected" in a muted style. This is information, not a control: it must not look tappable, no chevron, no border.

4. Section "Card Colour". A small bold label, then a horizontal row of five circular swatches, each a 48px touch target, in exactly these colours: #E42424, #0C3C84, #0C84CC, #0C843C, #FCCC0C. The third one is selected and shows a selection ring plus a small check. These five are the fallback colour for a card that has no brand logo.

5. A row reading "Add to favourites" on the left with a switch on the right, separated from the fields above by a hairline rule.

FOOTER
A full-width primary button labelled "Save card": ink #181824 fill, white text, 52px tall, 12px radius. It sits in the page flow above the safe area with a 20px margin — it does not float and it is not a FAB.

TONE
This screen carries no brand colour at all, so it must stay quiet: cream #F0F0E8 ground, white input surfaces, ink #181824 text, hairline outlines, flat — no shadows, no gradients. The signature yellow #FCCC0C appears nowhere here except as one of the five colour swatches. Headline in Space Grotesk, everything else Inter.
